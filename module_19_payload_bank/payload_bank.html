The XSS Payload Bank — 100+ Annotated Attacks

Every payload is a hypothesis about a broken assumption. This bank teaches you to read a payload and instantly know: what context it targets, which mechanism executes it, what defense it bypasses, and what would stop it. It is your lifelong field guide and your Module 18 evaluation corpus.

How to use it. Filter by keyword, or open a category. Copy any payload and fire it at the matching lab endpoint (e.g. mechanism-1/2 → Module 04's /search; DOM sinks → Module 09's SPA; filter evasion → Module 12's /waf-search). Each entry is tagged by the five-mechanism taxonomy so you can measure a defense's coverage by mechanism, not by payload count. Payloads are shown as inert, escaped text — nothing here executes.
